The Mathematics of Infinity

1. The Borel Normal Number Conjecture:
An irrational number is said to be "normal" in base 10 if all finite sequences of length k appear with uniform asymptotic frequency (1 in 10^k). While a formal proof is one of the grand open problems of mathematics, tests across trillions of digits confirm π behaves as an ideal normal number. This means every date in human history is mathematically guaranteed to occur inside π.


2. Combinatorial Frequency & Depth:
• 4-Digit Sequences (10^4 = 10,000 combinations): Expected within the first ~10,000 digits.
• 6-Digit Sequences (10^6 = 1,000,000 combinations): Expected within the first ~1-3 million digits.
• 8-Digit Sequences (10^8 = 100,000,000 combinations): Expected within the first 100-200 million digits.


3. The Bailey–Borwein–Plouffe (BBP) Formula:
Discovered in 1995, the BBP formula allows calculating arbitrary n-th hexadecimal digits of π directly in O(n log n) time without computing preceding digits, unlocking massive GPU concurrency.
